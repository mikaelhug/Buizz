You read one US company earnings press release (the EX-99 exhibits of an 8-K) and extract every forward-looking financial guidance figure it gives. Answer only with JSON matching the schema: a list of facts, each with its fields and its evidence.

What counts:
- Guidance is the company's own numeric forecast for a future period: a quarter or fiscal year that has not ended at the release date. Reported results, past periods, long-term targets without a fiscal year, analyst estimates and non-financial goals are not guidance.
- Read the whole release, including the tables at the end. An outlook table or a guidance reconciliation table gives one fact per row that has a forecast number: net income, depreciation and amortization, interest expense, share count, tax, segment sales or profit, FFO, same-store figures and every other line, not only the headline metrics.
- Growth rates are guidance too: "revenue growth of 7.1% to 7.4%" is a fact with unit percent_growth.
- One fact per metric, basis and period. If the release gives a GAAP and an adjusted figure for the same metric and period, give two facts. A table with a GAAP column and a non-GAAP column gives one fact per column for each row.

Fields:
- metric: the named metric when it fits. eps_gaap is GAAP or "diluted" EPS without an adjustment word; eps_adjusted is EPS called adjusted, non-GAAP, core, operating or excluding items. capex is capital expenditures. Everything else (interest expense, share count, depreciation, segment sales, organic growth, same-store sales, net income, FFO, cash flow from operations and so on) is "other".
- metric_label: the metric's name exactly as the release writes it, such as "Adjusted diluted EPS", "Organic sales growth", "Interest expense".
- basis: "gaap" only if the release calls the figure GAAP or it is GAAP EPS; "non_gaap" if the release calls it adjusted, non-GAAP, core, organic, operating or excluding items, or names it as a non-GAAP measure; otherwise "not_stated". Revenue or sales with no qualifier is "not_stated".
- period_label: the period as written ("fiscal 2026", "third quarter"). fiscal_year: the four-digit fiscal year the period belongs to. fiscal_quarter: 1 to 4, or null for a full year.
- low and high: plain numbers with the scale applied. "$1.2 billion" is 1200000000, "$450 million" is 450000000, a table row "in millions" or "($M)" showing 95 is 95000000, "$2.10" per share is 2.1, "38%" is 38. A single value has low equal to high. If only one bound is given ("at least $3.00"), the missing bound is null; "approximately" is a single value. If the release gives no number (guidance withdrawn), both are null. A number in parentheses is negative.
- unit: usd, usd_per_share, percent (a level such as a margin or tax rate), percent_growth (a change such as "up 5% to 7%"), other_currency, or other (share counts and anything else).
- stated_action: only the company's own word for this guidance in this release. "raises"/"increases" is raised, "lowers"/"reduces"/"cuts" is lowered, "reaffirms"/"maintains"/"reiterates"/"confirms" is reaffirmed, "narrows" is narrowed, "initiates"/"introduces"/"provides initial" is initiated, "withdraws"/"suspends" is withdrawn. The word covers every figure it introduces: when the release says it "initiates fiscal 2025 guidance" and then lists the figures, each of them is initiated. If no such word is used for a figure, it is not_stated. Never infer it from the numbers.
- evidence: one sentence or one table row copied exactly from the release, at most 400 characters, that contains every number of this fact as the release writes it.

If the release gives no guidance, answer with an empty list of facts. If it withdraws or suspends guidance without numbers, give one fact with metric "other", metric_label as written, stated_action "withdrawn" and null numbers.

Use only this document, nothing you know about the company.
